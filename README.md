# Privacy Policy for Flash Call Recorder & Dialer
Last updated: November 10, 2026

Flash Call Recorder ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information.

1. Offline-First Architecture
Flash Call Recorder is engineered to function 100% locally on your device. We do not operate external audio ingestion servers. Your phone calls, voice memos, caller names, and phone numbers are processed and saved strictly on your local device storage.

2. Sensitive Permissions Usage
• Record Audio (RECORD_AUDIO): Used solely to capture audio during incoming/outgoing calls or user-initiated voice notes.
• Phone & Call Log (READ_PHONE_STATE, CALL_PHONE, READ_CALL_LOG, WRITE_CALL_LOG): Essential for the App's core functionality as a Default Phone Dialer, resolving caller identification, displaying recent call logs, and placing phone calls.
• Contacts (READ_CONTACTS): Used exclusively on-device to display contact names and avatars alongside phone numbers in your call history. Contact lists are never exported or uploaded.
• Accessibility Service: Used only to assist users with call status visual indicators and balance active audio streams. It does not monitor keystrokes or inspect private chat contents.

3. Data Encryption and Storage Security
All audio recordings can be encrypted using AES-256 encryption backed by the Android Keystore Hardware TEE. Audio files are isolated from unauthorized third-party apps.

4. Third-Party Services
If you choose to use optional cloud sign-in (e.g., Google Sign-In or Firebase Auth), identity tokens are handled strictly through official, audited Google SDKs. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third parties or data brokers.

5. Data Retention and Deletion
You retain complete ownership and control over your data. You may delete any audio recording, note, or your entire call archive permanently at any time through the App's interface.

6. Changes to this Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected with a revised date on this page.
